Frequently Asked Questions

Rentool

How do I list a tool for rent?
Create an account, tap "List a Tool," add photos and details, set your price, and publish.

How are payments handled?
Payments are processed securely through the app. Owners receive payouts after the rental period ends.

What if something gets damaged?
Contact the other party through the app first. If you can't resolve it, reach out to our support team.

FlightDose

How accurate are the radiation estimates?
Estimates are based on established atmospheric radiation models. They are intended for informational purposes and may vary from actual exposure.

Does FlightDose work offline?
You need an internet connection to fetch flight and radiation data. Previously viewed flights are cached for offline access.
