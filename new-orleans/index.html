06 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you
reach out.

Do I need to know much about technology?

No. You know your business, and that's what we need. We translate it into technology and explain every recommendation in plain terms.

Is this only for AI projects?

No. AI is one of the tools we use, and we use it where it clearly pays off. Plenty of problems are better solved with conventional software, an integration, or a change to how you use the tools you already have. The assessment tells you which is which.

What does the assessment cost?

$750, fixed. If you hire us for a build based on the plan, the full fee is credited toward it. Either way, the written plan is yours to keep.

How are builds priced?

Each build is quoted as a fixed price from the assessment plan, with the deliverables and schedule agreed in writing before work begins. Small projects don't need a full assessment, and we'll tell you on the intro call if yours is one of them.

Will my customer data be sent to AI companies?

Only if you choose that. We design every AI solution around what your data requires, including private and locally run models, and we never put customer information into public chatbots.

Who owns what you build?

You do. Ownership, source code, and account terms are agreed in writing before work begins, and everything runs on accounts in your business's name.

What size business do you work with?

Small and mid-sized businesses, from a single location with a handful of staff to multi-location operations.

Do you work outside the New Orleans metro?

Not for these services. On-site work is limited to Orleans, Jefferson, St. Tammany, St. Bernard, St. Charles, St. John the Baptist, and Plaquemines parishes.
