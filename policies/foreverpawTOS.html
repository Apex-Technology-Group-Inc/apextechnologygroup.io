15. Cancelling While Over the Free Limit

Suppose you are storing 40 GB and you cancel. You are now far above the free allowance. Here is exactly what happens, and it is the same whether you cancelled deliberately, let a card expire, or had a complimentary grant withdrawn.

Nothing is destroyed and nothing is hidden.

  • You keep every photograph, video, letter, and story.
  • You can continue to view, share, and export all of it.
  • What stops is adding: new media uploads are blocked while you are over the limit, with a clear notice in the app explaining why.
  • You have at least 90 days, with email reminders sent well before the deadline rather than on it, to either resubscribe or download your media.
  • At the end of the 90 days, nothing is deleted. Media above your free allowance is moved to long-term cold storage instead, exactly as described in Section 16: thumbnails stay online, your whole timeline still opens and reads normally, and full-resolution originals are marked as restorable.
  • Restoring is free and available whenever you want it — automatically if you resubscribe, or on request if you do not. As in Section 16, restoring takes hours rather than seconds, and we will tell you so and email you when it is done.
  • The account remains unable to add new media until it is under the free limit or subscribed again.

There is no point in this process at which your content is deleted.

We considered deleting oldest-first down to the limit, which is what many services do, and rejected it. It destroys real memories on a schedule, and we are not willing to be the product that deleted your dog’s first year because a subscription lapsed. Moving those files to slower storage costs you nothing but patience on the day you want them back, and it is the reason we can promise never to delete them.

16. Inactive Accounts and Archival

WE DO NOT DELETE YOUR CONTENT FOR INACTIVITY. THERE IS NO INACTIVITY DELETION RULE IN FOREVER PAW.

Why this term exists in the form it does.

The person most likely to stop opening Forever Paw is the person whose dog has died. They are also the person whose timeline matters most, and the one least able to recreate it. A policy that deleted content after a period of inactivity would fall hardest on exactly them, quietly, months later, while they were not looking. So the rule is archival, and it has no end state in which anything is destroyed.

If you stop signing in

  • At about 6 months, we email you. Reminders continue and become more frequent as the archive date approaches, so this never happens without warning.
  • At about 9 months, full-resolution photographs and videos are moved to long-term cold storage. Thumbnails stay online — your whole timeline still opens and reads normally, with originals marked as restorable.
  • Whenever you return — a month later or a decade later — we offer to restore your originals at no charge to you.

Restoring is not instant. It takes hours rather than seconds, and the app will tell you so and email you when it is done, rather than leaving you watching a spinner.

Paying subscribers are never archived

If you hold an active subscription, your media is never moved to cold storage and never deleted, no matter how long it has been since you last opened the app.

You are paying us to keep it immediately available, and we keep it immediately available. You will still receive the six-month message, because a year of silent billing for something you are not opening is a thing you deserve to be told about, and it will tell you how to cancel as well as how to come back.

The limits of this commitment

This section is a commitment about our policy, not a warranty against every possible outcome. It does not override Sections 23 through 25, and it does not apply where we are required to delete data by law, where an account is terminated for breach of Section 9, or where you delete your own content or account. Data loss can still occur for the reasons in Section 17, which is why you should keep your originals.
