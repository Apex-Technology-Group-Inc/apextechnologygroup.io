Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 7 May 2026

Company: FlightDose, operated by Apex Technology Group, Inc.

1. Overview

FlightDose respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains exactly what information we collect, why we collect it, which third parties process it on our behalf, and the choices you have over your data.

We do not sell your personal data.
We do not share your flight or dose history with advertisers.
We collect only what is necessary to operate the FlightDose service.

2. Information We Collect

A. Account Information

When you sign in to FlightDose using Google Sign-In, the following information is provided to us by Google and stored through Firebase Authentication so we can authenticate you on subsequent launches:

  • Your name as set on your Google account
  • Your email address
  • Your Google profile photo URL (used to display your avatar in the app)
  • A unique Google account identifier and a Firebase user ID
  • Authentication tokens used to verify your session

B. Flight & Dose Data

When you use the app, we store the following on our servers:

  • Flights you log (departure airport, arrival airport, dates, and times)
  • CARI-7 cosmic radiation dose estimates calculated from your flights
  • Cumulative dose totals over time
  • Achievements and accolades you earn within the app
  • Flight groups you create or join (group name and membership)
  • Whether you have purchased the optional "Remove Ads" upgrade

C. Purchase Information

If you purchase the optional "Remove Ads" upgrade, we receive limited information from Apple's App Store via StoreKit:

  • The product identifier purchased (remove_ads)
  • A transaction identifier used to verify your entitlement

We do not receive your credit card number, billing address, Apple ID password, or any other payment instrument details. All payment processing is handled directly by Apple.

D. Device & Advertising Data (via Google AdMob)

If you have not purchased the ad-free upgrade, the app displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect the following directly from your device for the purpose of serving ads:

  • A device-scoped advertising identifier (IDFV) used for frequency capping and basic ad delivery
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address (city or region level)
  • Device type, operating system version, and language
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, view duration)

FlightDose does not request access to Apple's IDFA via the App Tracking Transparency framework, and we do not link AdMob data to your FlightDose account, your flight history, or your dose records. Data collected by AdMob is governed by Google's privacy policy and ad-technology terms (linked in Section 4).

E. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not access your device's precise location (GPS).
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, calendar, microphone, camera, or other on-device personal data.
  • We do not collect health data from Apple Health or any other health platform.
  • We do not collect or transmit analytics events, crash reports, or diagnostic telemetry beyond what is required to authenticate you and serve the app.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your data exclusively to:

  • Authenticate your account and keep your sign-in session active
  • Calculate and display your CARI-7 radiation dose estimates
  • Maintain a running record of your cumulative dose and flight history
  • Award and display achievements based on your in-app activity
  • Enable shared flight groups among users you choose to connect with
  • Verify ad-free entitlement for users who have purchased the upgrade
  • Display advertisements (free tier only, served by Google AdMob)

We do not sell, rent, or share your flight or dose history for marketing purposes.

5. Flight Groups & Sharing With Other Users

When you create or join a flight group, other members of that group may be able to view information you have chosen to share with the group, such as your display name and aggregated flight or dose statistics. You can leave a group at any time, after which no further information is shared with its remaining members.

FlightDose does not make any user data publicly searchable or visible outside the groups you have explicitly joined.

6. Data Storage & Security

Your account and flight data are stored on secure servers operated on behalf of Apex Technology Group, Inc. Authentication is handled through Google Firebase infrastructure. All data in transit between the FlightDose app and our servers is protected with industry-standard TLS encryption.

We implement reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your data from unauthorized access or disclosure. However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain your account, flight history, and dose records for as long as your account remains active. If your account becomes inactive for an extended period, we may delete inactive data after providing reasonable notice to your registered email address. You may request deletion at any time, as described in Section 8.

8. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your account and all associated data
  • Export a copy of your flight and dose history
  • Withdraw consent for any optional processing

To exercise any of these rights, email flightdose.dev@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

When you request deletion, we will permanently remove:

  • Your account and authentication records
  • Flight history and CARI-7 dose records
  • Cumulative dose totals and achievements
  • Flight group memberships you have created or joined

Once deleted, this data cannot be recovered.

9. Regional Notices

California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, request deletion of their information, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. FlightDose does not sell personal information and does not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. To exercise CCPA / CPRA rights, contact us at the email address in Section 11.

European Economic Area, United Kingdom & Switzerland (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are located in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, our legal bases for processing your personal data are:

  • Contract — to provide the FlightDose service you signed up for
  • Legitimate interest — to operate, secure, and improve the service
  • Consent — for any optional features you explicitly enable

Apex Technology Group, Inc. is the data controller for personal data processed through FlightDose. You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

10. Children's Privacy

FlightDose is not directed toward children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will promptly delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. If we make material changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you within the app or by email. Continued use of the app after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
