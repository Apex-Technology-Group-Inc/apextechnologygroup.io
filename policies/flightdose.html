Privacy Policy

Effective Date:24-February-2026

Company: FlightDose (operated by Apex Technology Group, Inc.)

1. Overview

FlightDose respects your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the limited circumstances in which third parties may collect data when you use our app or services.

We collect only what is necessary to provide the FlightDose service.
We do not sell your data.
We do not share your personal data with third parties.

2. Information We Collect

A. Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

  • Username
  • Email address (if applicable)
  • Authentication information (via Firebase or other authentication provider)

B. Flight & Dose Data

When you use the app, we store:

  • Flight history entered by you
  • CARI-7 dose calculations generated by the app
  • Running cumulative dose records
  • Dates and associated metadata related to dose entries

This data is stored solely to provide you with:

  • Historical tracking
  • Cumulative dose calculations
  • Long-term record keeping

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your data exclusively to:

  • Calculate CARI-7 radiation dose estimates
  • Maintain a running record of your cumulative dose
  • Authenticate your account
  • Improve app stability and functionality

We do not sell, rent, or share your flight or dose history for marketing purposes.

4. Data Sharing

FlightDose does not sell or share your personal flight or dose data with third parties.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app may display advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain device or usage information as part of its standard advertising services.

FlightDose:

  • Does not provide your flight history or dose records to AdMob
  • Does not share personal radiation data with advertisers
  • Only integrates AdMob to the extent required for ad functionality

You can review Google’s privacy policy at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure servers and is accessible only for the purpose of providing the FlightDose service.

We implement reasonable safeguards to protect your data from unauthorized access or disclosure. However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account and dose records for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion at any time.

7. Account & Data Deletion

If you wish to permanently delete your account and all associated data:

  • Contact us at: flightdose.dev@gmail.com

We will permanently delete:

  • Flight history
  • CARI-7 dose records
  • Cumulative dose totals
  • Account information

Once deleted, this data cannot be recovered.

8. Children’s Privacy

FlightDose is not directed toward children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. If we do, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
