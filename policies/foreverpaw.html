Forever Paw Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 23 August 2026

Last Updated: 23 August 2026

Application: Forever Paw

Company: Apex Technology Group, Inc. (“Apex,” “Forever Paw,” “we,” “us,” “our”)

Forever Paw pairs short daily training with a timeline of your dog’s life. To do that it stores what you put into it — your dog’s profile, your photographs, your videos, your recordings, and the things you write. This policy explains exactly what we hold, where it goes, how long we keep it, and how to get it back or delete it.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your photographs or your writing to train AI models. We never show advertising to subscribers, and we never target advertising using your dog’s timeline.

1. Scope

This policy covers the Forever Paw mobile applications and the Forever Paw API operated by Apex Technology Group, Inc., a Delaware corporation. Apex is the controller of the personal data described here.

Forever Paw is about a dog, but the data is about you — your account, your photographs, your words, and where you went. We treat it that way throughout.

2. What we collect

A. Account information

You sign in with Apple or with Google, through Firebase Authentication. From that we receive and store:

  • Your email address (or the private relay address, if you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email)
  • Your display name, if your provider supplies one
  • Your profile photo URL, if your provider supplies one
  • A Firebase user identifier, which we use as your account key
  • Account creation and last sign-in timestamps

We never receive or store your Apple ID or Google password. Your last sign-in time matters more than it looks — it is what the idle-account lifecycle in Section 10 runs on.

B. Your preferences

Your daily reminder time and your IANA time zone. The time zone is necessary because streaks are counted in your local days, and a reminder sent at the wrong local hour is worse than no reminder.

C. Your dog’s profile

Name, photograph, breed and whether mixed, birth date, gotcha day, size, energy level, temperament tags, current skills, and the training goals you choose. Also whether the dog is active or in Forever Mode, and an optional date of passing if you choose to record one.

D. Memories — the timeline

This is the heart of the product and the most personal data we hold:

  • Photographs you upload, and thumbnails we generate from them
  • Videos you upload
  • Audio recordings, including the voice recordings attached to “Letters to My Dog”
  • Titles, captions, and body text you write
  • The date each memory occurred, and whether it was created by you or automatically by a milestone

E. Places

A memory may optionally carry a place name and coordinates. This is per-memory and opt-in: a place is attached only to a memory you are saving, only when you choose to attach one.

Forever Paw does not track your location in the background, does not build a movement history, and does not record where you are except when you deliberately tag a memory. Location is never used for advertising.

Where the app asks your device for a location to help you tag a place, it asks at that moment and you can decline and type a place name instead, or attach no place at all. Places you have tagged are rolled up into a per-dog list, and place names are used in AI recaps (Section 5). Coordinates are not sent to the AI provider.

F. Training activity

Which programme your dog is enrolled in, every challenge you complete, how it went, the local date it happened, your streak, and the achievements your dog has earned. Outcomes are also used to adapt the next session’s difficulty.

G. Personality answers

Your answers to the twelve prompts about what makes your dog theirs.

H. Medication and vaccine records

If you use the reminder feature: the medication or vaccine name you enter, the schedule you set, doses you record as given, and the resulting history. This is information about your dog, entered by you.

Forever Paw is not veterinary advice. It keeps track of what you enter and reminds you when you ask it to. It never interprets a drug, checks a dose, or warns you about anything. Always follow your vet’s instructions and contact them with any question about your dog’s health or medication.

I. Devices and notifications

A push notification token for each device you sign in on, its platform (iOS or Android), and when it was registered and last seen. Tokens are stored on your account record so that deleting your account takes them with it.

J. Subscription and purchase information

If you subscribe to Forever Paw+, we store a record of your entitlement: the product you bought (monthly or yearly), its status, the current period dates, and identifiers Apple or Google provide so we can verify and maintain it. We also receive server notifications from the app store about renewal, cancellation, refund, billing retry, and grace period, so your access matches what you have actually paid for.

We never receive your payment card number, billing address, or store account password. All payment processing is handled by Apple or Google.

Some accounts are marked as complimentary by us (for testing, support, or as a gift). That is a flag on your account and involves no payment data at all.

K. Usage accounting

We keep two internal ledgers so that limits can be enforced fairly:

  • AI usage — which feature you invoked, when, how many tokens or images it consumed, and the estimated cost. It records the shape of a call, not its content.
  • Stored media — the size of each object you store, so we can show your storage against your plan’s cap and enforce it.

L. Advertising data (free tier only)

If you are on the free tier, the app shows occasional full-screen advertisements served by Google AdMob when you submit content. AdMob collects data directly from your device to deliver and measure those ads, which may include:

  • A device advertising identifier
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address (city or region level — not your device GPS, and not the places you tag)
  • Device type, operating system version, and language
  • Ad interaction data such as impressions, clicks, and view duration

No Forever Paw content is ever used for advertising. AdMob does not receive your dog’s profile, your photographs, your videos, your recordings, your memories, your tagged places, your training history, or your medication records. We do not link ad data to your Forever Paw account.

Subscribers see no advertising at all, and the ad SDK is not asked to serve anything for a subscriber. Section 6 explains your choices.

M. Email and support correspondence

We use your email address to send account and service messages, including the idle-account notices described in Section 10, and to reply when you contact support. If you email us, we hold your message and address so we can respond.

N. Technical and diagnostic data

Ordinary server logs necessary to operate and secure the service — request paths, status codes, timing, and error diagnostics. We do not run a third-party product-analytics SDK or a session-replay tool.

3. What we do not collect

  • No background or continuous location. See Section 2(E).
  • No facial recognition or biometric identification of people or of dogs. We do not detect, match, or index faces in your photographs.
  • No contacts, calendar, or SMS access, and no microphone access beyond a recording you deliberately make for a letter.
  • No human health data. Forever Paw does not read from or write to Apple Health, Google Health Connect, or any other health platform.
  • No payment card details.
  • No data brokers. We buy no data about you.
  • No use of your content for advertising or for AI training.

Photo metadata

We strip EXIF metadata from every image at upload, before storing it.

Phone photographs routinely carry the GPS coordinates, device model, and timestamp of where and how they were taken. We remove that metadata rather than storing it, so a photograph in your timeline does not silently carry your home address. Orientation is applied to the image first so the picture still looks right.

5. AI features and OpenAI

Several features are produced by a third-party AI provider, OpenAI: story recaps, weekly progress summaries, suggested captions, memory prompts, and caricature or painted portraits. This section says exactly what leaves our servers.

What is sent for text features

When you generate a recap, a digest, a caption, or a memory prompt, we send:

  • Your dog’s name, breed, birth date, and gotcha day
  • The period covered, and counts of training sessions completed
  • Milestone names your dog reached in that period
  • The memories you saved in that period — their dates, titles, and the body text you wrote
  • Place names you attached to those memories, and how often you visited each
  • Your personality answers

What is sent for portraits

The photograph you select. It is resized and its EXIF metadata is stripped before it leaves our server.

What is never sent

  • Your name, your email address, or your account identifier
  • Your device push tokens or any advertising identifier
  • Place coordinates — only the place name you typed or chose
  • Your videos or your audio recordings
  • Your medication records
  • Your subscription or payment information

Terms that apply

  • We use OpenAI’s API under its business terms. OpenAI does not use data submitted through the API to train its models under those terms.
  • OpenAI may retain API inputs and outputs for a limited period for abuse monitoring, per its own policy, and then delete them.
  • OpenAI’s handling of what it receives is governed by its policies: privacy policy and terms.

Your control

AI features are optional. If you never generate a recap, a caption, a memory prompt, or a portrait, and you turn off scheduled recaps and digests in your settings, none of your content is sent to an AI provider at all. Forever Paw’s training, timeline, letters, and reminders all work without it.

AI output can be wrong. A recap may misdescribe an event, get a detail backwards, or write something that reads oddly about a dog you love. A portrait is an artistic generation, not a likeness. Nothing produced by these features is a factual record — your own memories and photographs are. You can delete any generated story, digest, or portrait.

7. How photos, videos, and recordings are stored and served

Media is stored in a private Amazon S3 bucket in the United States. The bucket is not world-readable, and files are streamed to the app through our API rather than served directly from the bucket.

Each stored file has an unguessable key containing a random component. Knowing your dog’s identifier is not enough to find its photographs.

An honest limitation you should know about. Media URLs are unguessable but not individually authenticated. This is the same trade every photo host makes: an image loading in a timeline cannot attach a sign-in token, so requiring one would break every picture in the app.

The practical consequences are:

  • Anyone you give a media URL to can open it without signing in.
  • A URL keeps working until the underlying file is removed — including for a short period after you delete the memory from your timeline.
  • The same protection currently applies to voice recordings as to photographs. We consider a recorded voice to deserve more, and signed, expiring URLs for audio are a planned improvement rather than a shipped one. We would rather tell you that than let you assume otherwise.

Media is not encrypted with a key only you hold. Apex personnel with production access could technically view stored files; access is restricted to the minimum personnel needed to operate the service, and we do not browse user content.

8. Notifications and emails

With your permission, we send push notifications: training reminders at your chosen local time, medication reminders when a dose is due, and milestone notifications such as birthdays and gotcha days. Delivery is through Apple Push Notification service and Firebase Cloud Messaging. You can turn these off in the app or your device settings at any time.

We also send email, through Amazon SES, for: account and security matters, billing and subscription changes, and the idle-account notices in Section 10. Service and lifecycle emails are not marketing, and you cannot opt out of them while you hold an account — an email warning you that your photographs are about to be archived is one you should receive.

Do not rely on a reminder for anything that matters medically. Push delivery is best-effort and depends on your device, your network, your notification settings, and third-party infrastructure we do not control. A reminder can be delayed or never arrive. Use your vet’s instructions as the source of truth.

10. Retention, idle accounts, and archival

We do not delete your dog’s photographs because you stopped using the app. Not at nine months, not at nine years.

That is a deliberate commitment, and this section explains exactly what does happen instead, because “we keep it forever” would be a promise with a cost attached and you should see the mechanism.

Why the rule is archival and not deletion.

The person most likely to stop opening Forever Paw is the person whose dog has died. They are also the person whose timeline matters most, and the one least able to recreate it. An inactivity rule that deleted content would fall hardest on exactly them, and it would do it quietly, months after the worst day of their year, while they were not looking.

So there is no inactivity deletion rule in Forever Paw. Long-unopened media is moved to cheaper storage and waits. If you come back in five years — or your family does — it is still there.

While your account is active

We keep your account, your dog’s profile, your timeline, and your media for as long as your account exists. A timeline that expires after ninety days is not a timeline.

If you stop signing in

Time since last sign-inWhat happens
6 months We email you. Reminders continue, becoming more frequent as the archive date approaches, so you are never surprised.
9 months Full-resolution photographs and videos are moved to long-term cold storage (Amazon S3 Glacier Deep Archive). Thumbnails stay online. Your whole timeline — every memory, date, caption, and small picture — still opens and still reads normally.
Whenever you come back We offer to restore your originals, at no charge to you.

Restoring from cold storage is not instant — it takes hours, not seconds. The app will tell you plainly and email you when your originals are back, rather than showing you a spinner.

Subscribers are never archived. If you have an active subscription your media stays immediately available regardless of how long it has been since you signed in. You will still get the six-month email, because a year of silent billing is its own problem and we would rather you know.

If you cancel a subscription while over the free storage limit

Nothing is deleted and nothing is hidden. You keep every photograph, video, letter, and story, and can view, share, and export all of them. What stops is adding new media until you are back under the free limit or subscribe again.

You get at least 90 days, with email reminders well beforehand, to resubscribe or download your media. After that, media above the free allowance is moved to long-term cold storage rather than deleted — thumbnails stay online, your timeline still opens and reads normally, and your originals are restorable free of charge whenever you ask or resubscribe. As with any restore from cold storage, it takes hours rather than seconds. Section 15 of the Terms of Service has the detail.

Other retention periods

  • Server logs and diagnostics: retained for a short operational period, then rotated out.
  • Usage ledgers (AI and storage): retained while needed for allowance accounting, billing accuracy, and cost analysis.
  • Subscription records: retained as long as needed for entitlement, and afterwards as required for tax, accounting, and dispute resolution.
  • Deleted memories and media: removed from your timeline immediately, and the underlying files are removed from storage; copies may persist briefly in routine backups until they expire, within thirty (30) days.

11. Your rights and how to use them

Export — built in

Forever Paw can hand you your whole account: your user record, your dogs, every memory, training sessions, earned achievements, personality answers, stories, digests, portraits, and places. Use the export option in the app, or ask us.

Delete — built in

You can delete your account from within the app. This permanently removes your account record, your dogs, your memories and their media, your training history, your personality answers, your stories and digests, your medication records, your usage ledgers, and your push tokens.

Deletion is permanent. Export first if you want to keep your dog’s timeline. Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription — cancel that through Apple or Google.

Other rights

Subject to applicable law, you may also:

  • Correct inaccurate data — most of it is editable in the app
  • Object to or restrict certain processing

  • Withdraw consent for notifications, location tagging, personalised advertising, or AI features, without losing the rest of the app
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising a right. Offering a paid tier with more features is not discrimination; refusing you service for making a privacy request would be, and we do not do it.
  • Appeal a decision we make on a request

Email apex@apextechnologygroup.io with “Privacy Request — Forever Paw” in the subject line. We verify requests through the email address on your account and respond within 45 days, extendable once where the law permits and we tell you why. If we decline, we will explain why and how to appeal; we decide appeals within 45 days and, on denial, tell you how to contact your state Attorney General.

12. Security

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is protected with TLS.
  • Every dog-scoped request verifies ownership; another account’s dog returns a 403.
  • The media bucket is private, and media keys carry a random component (Section 7).
  • Entitlement is resolved on our servers, never trusted from the app, so a modified client cannot grant itself a plan or read another account’s data.
  • Credentials and service keys are held outside the application directory on the server and are never committed to source control.
  • Access to production systems is limited to the minimum personnel needed to operate them.
  • Security headers, HTTPS redirection, HSTS, rate limiting, and request size limits are enforced at the API.

No system is perfectly secure. If we discover a breach affecting your personal data, we will notify you and any regulator entitled to notice, within the timeframes applicable law requires.

To report a vulnerability, email apex@apextechnologygroup.io. We will not pursue legal action against good-faith security research that respects other users’ privacy, avoids service degradation and data destruction, uses only accounts you control, and gives us reasonable time to fix an issue before disclosure.
