This is a separate document from our Privacy Policy, provided with its own dedicated link as required by the Washington My Health My Data Act (RCW 19.373). It describes how we handle consumer health data specifically.

It applies to residents of Washington and Nevada, and we apply the same commitments to everyone, everywhere, because doing otherwise would mean building two products. It also covers the consumer health data provisions of Connecticut, New York, and other states with comparable requirements.

Where this policy addresses consumer health data, it controls over the general Privacy Policy and over the Terms of Service.

MYSIEVERT IS NOT MEDICAL ADVICE AND INTERPRETS NOTHING.

MySievert records medical procedures and displays typical published radiation dose values for them. It does not diagnose, treat, cure, mitigate, or prevent any disease; it does not provide medical advice; and it does not tell you whether any dose is safe, unsafe, high, low, normal, elevated, or worth worrying about — it performs no evaluation of your data at all. Speak to your doctor, radiologist, or a qualified medical physicist about your radiation exposure. See Sections 3, 4, and 5 of the Terms of Service.

1. The essential facts

Your consumer health data is encrypted on your device with a key we do not hold and cannot obtain. We cannot read it, list it, count it, or describe it. Not with database access, not under legal compulsion, and not at your request.

  • We do not sell consumer health data. Never have, do not, and will not.
  • We do not share consumer health data with anyone except the processors listed in Section 6, which receive only ciphertext they cannot read.
  • We do not use consumer health data for advertising. There is no advertising in this product at all.
  • We never geofence. The app has never requested and will never request location permission, for any reason.
  • We collect only what you choose to enter, and only after you consent.

2. What counts as consumer health data here

Under Washington law, “consumer health data” is personal information linked or reasonably linkable to a consumer that identifies their past, present, or future physical or mental health status. In MySievert, that is:

Precise location data is not collected under any circumstances. The application requests no location permission of any kind. It therefore cannot, and does not, infer anything from your proximity to a healthcare facility.

Biometric data is not collected. Device unlock via fingerprint or face is performed by your operating system; we never receive biometric information.

3. Sources of consumer health data

There is exactly one source.

You. Everything in your history is there because you typed it in.

We do not obtain consumer health data from:

  • Healthcare providers, hospitals, imaging facilities, or laboratories
  • Health plans, insurers, or claims clearinghouses
  • Health information exchanges or interoperability networks
  • Pharmacies or pharmacy benefit managers
  • Apple Health, Google Health Connect, wearables, or any other health platform
  • Data brokers, advertising networks, or list vendors
  • Public records, social media, or web tracking of any kind

We also do not derive or infer consumer health data from anything other than what you enter. We perform no inference, profiling, scoring, or interpretation over your records — and as our servers cannot read them, we could not do so even if we wanted to.

4. Why we collect it, and how it is used

The purpose of collecting your consumer health data is to provide the record-keeping service you asked for: to store your radiation history, keep it available across your devices, and display it back to you. That is the whole purpose.

The specific uses are:

  • On your device: displaying your history, attaching published reference dose values from our catalog, computing running totals, rendering the body diagram, and producing a PDF export when you ask for one.
  • On our servers: storing your encrypted data so you do not lose it when you change or lose a device, and returning it to you when you sign in on a new one.

We do not use your consumer health data for advertising, marketing, profiling, research, product analytics, model training, scoring, or resale. We cannot use it for anything at all, because we cannot read it.

We do not process consumer health data for any purpose that is neither disclosed here nor reasonably necessary to and compatible with the purpose for which you provided it. If that ever changes, we will obtain your consent first.

5. Consent

Consent to collect

Before you create your first health record, the app asks for your clear, affirmative, freely given, specific, informed, opt-in consent to collect consumer health data for the purposes in Section 4. Consent is not bundled with anything else, is not a pre-checked box, is not obtained through a dark pattern or a design that nudges you toward accepting, and is not a condition of downloading or opening the app. We record the fact and time of your consent.

Separate consent to share

Sharing consumer health data requires a separate consent, obtained separately from the consent to collect.

We do not share consumer health data, so we have never asked for this consent and do not expect to. If that ever changed, we would ask separately, specifically, and in advance — and you could decline and keep using the app exactly as before.

Sale

We do not sell consumer health data. Selling it would require your signed, written authorisation containing all elements the law requires; we have never sought such an authorisation and have no intention of doing so.

Withdrawing consent

You may withdraw consent at any time, in the app or by emailing us. Withdrawal stops further collection. It does not by itself delete what you have already stored — use the deletion right in Section 7 for that, which you can exercise at the same time and which we will treat as a combined request if you say so.

With respect to your consumer health data, you have the right to:

  • Confirm whether we are collecting, sharing, or selling your consumer health data, and to access it.
  • Obtain a list of all third parties and affiliates with whom we have shared or to whom we have sold your consumer health data, together with contact information for each. Our current answer is: none, other than the processors named in Section 6.
  • Withdraw consent to collection and to sharing.
  • Delete your consumer health data.
  • Appeal a refusal to act on any of the above.
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising any of these rights. We will not deny you the service, charge you a different price, or provide a lower quality experience because you exercised a right.

How to make a request

Email apex@apextechnologygroup.io with the subject line “Consumer Health Data Request — MySievert”, or use the corresponding control in the app. Deletion is also available directly in the app.

We will respond within 45 days, extendable once by a further 45 days where reasonably necessary, in which case we will tell you within the first 45 days and explain why. An authorised agent may act on your behalf with proof of authority.

What deletion actually does

On a verified deletion request we delete your consumer health data from our production systems, and we notify all processors and affiliates that hold it — those listed in Section 6 — and instruct them to do the same. Deletion propagates to backups and archived systems within thirty (30) days as they expire in the normal rotation.

Because we hold no derived data, no analytics rows, no activity table, and no record of how many entries you have, there is no residual copy anywhere for us to overlook.

Deletion is permanent and irreversible. Export your history from the app first if you want to keep it — we cannot export it for you, because we cannot read it.

Identity verification

We verify a request by requiring you to act from the account in question. We hold no name, email, or other identifying information on our servers with which to verify you any other way, and we will not ask you for additional personal information solely to verify a request. If we cannot authenticate a request, we will tell you so promptly and explain why.

Appeals

If we decline to act, we will tell you why within 45 days and explain how to appeal. To appeal, reply to that response or email us with “Appeal” in the subject line. We will decide the appeal within 45 days and give you a written explanation of our reasoning. If we deny the appeal, we will provide a method for you to contact the Washington State Attorney General’s Office to submit a complaint, or the corresponding authority in your own state.

8. Aggregate statistics, and why they are not about you

We maintain anonymous aggregate counts of which procedures are commonly logged across the platform, so we know which reference values to verify and refine first. These are population totals. They are not consumer health data, because they are not linked and are not reasonably linkable to any consumer.

That is a design property, enforced by four constraints:

  1. No IP address is retained anywhere in the chain. The client address is stripped from the connection by the first component in our request pipeline, before any logger, handler, or metric can observe it, and our build fails if a component that would record it is introduced. Nothing rate-limits, partitions, geolocates, or shards by address, because no address exists to use.
  2. Counts carry no authentication token, so there is no moment at which a counter and an account identity coexist in a request.
  3. One counter per report, never a set, because a set of procedures is a fingerprint that describes a person.
  4. Batched with jitter, so arrival time does not correspond to the moment you logged anything.

If any one of these four constraints ever ceased to hold, this policy would change in the same release, or the change would not ship. Aggregate counts are not deleted on a deletion request, because there is nothing in them to trace to you.

9. Geofencing

We do not operate, use, or permit any geofence around any healthcare facility, provider, pharmacy, or any other location, for any purpose — not to identify consumers, not to track them, not to collect data, and not to send notifications or advertisements.

This is not merely a policy commitment. The application does not request location permission of any kind, has never done so, and is subject to a permanent internal rule that it never will. There is no technical means by which we could operate a geofence, and building one would require deliberately violating a documented product constraint.

10. Security of consumer health data

Consumer health data is encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM under a 256-bit key that is generated on your device, never transmitted in unwrapped form, and never held by or derivable by any server we operate or any processor. Transport is TLS 1.2 or higher, applied over data that is already ciphertext. Full technical detail, including key derivation, key wrapping, and key custody, is in Section 7 of the Privacy Policy.

We restrict access to production systems to the minimum personnel necessary to operate them. Such access yields ciphertext. There is no administrative view of consumer health data, because no such view can exist.

The corresponding risk, stated plainly: if you lose both your device and your recovery code, your consumer health data becomes permanently unreadable, including by us. Please set up a recovery code and export a PDF copy of your history periodically.

11. Retention

We retain your encrypted consumer health data for as long as your account exists, because its purpose is to be a durable history you can return to. There is no other retention period, and no secondary or derived copy.

We delete it when you delete your account, when you make a deletion request, or when you withdraw consent and ask us to delete.

12. Children

MySievert is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect consumer health data from anyone under 18. An adult may record procedures relating to a person in their care within their own account, and is responsible for having the authority to do so.
